Sloppanizer is a keyboard-first organizer for todos, notes and the people behind them. Next up ranks your open work and tells you why.

Todos as flight strips

Every todo is one strip: a priority band on the left, the title, scoped labels such as priority::high (one value per scope), who asked for it and who to talk to. Due date and age sit in fixed columns, so a long list still reads at a glance. Done todos show how long they took.

  1. #203 Plan the network switch swap priorityhigh areanetwork requested by Jonas Brandt, contact Ines Berger 18.11. 12d
  2. #188 Order spare disks typepurchase requested by Anna Meier 02.12. 5d
  3. #174 Move the wiki to the new host areainfra done, took 3d 4h 08.11. done

Next up shows its reasons

Next up adds points for what matters and puts the result in order. Each strip says why it is where it is, so you can trust the order or overrule it.

priority::critical
The priority label you set.
overdue 2d
Past its due date, and by how much.
due in 3d
Due soon.
planned today
You planned it for today in the week view.
open 41d
Waiting a long time since it was made.
untouched 63d
Nobody has changed it for weeks.
blocks 2
Other todos wait for this one.

Whom do I ask about firewall rules?

People are part of the work: who asked for a todo and who knows the answer. Keep their team, the topics they know and your own private tags. The statistics show who asks for how much and how long it takes.

Notes, threads and files

Markdown everywhere, with a live preview. Each todo has a thread of comments next to its history. Paste a screenshot and it is stored with the todo. Checklists, subtasks, links between todos (blocks, blocked by, relates to) and repeating todos are built in. 

## Steps
- [x] Export the current config
- [x] Order the new switch #188
- [ ] Book a window with Ines
- [ ] Swap and test

Blocked by #188 repeats every month

Board and week planner

Move todos between columns on the board, or plan them on the days of your week. Drag them with the mouse, or use the keys: every move works from the keyboard too.

Keyboard first

Everything has a key, and ? lists them on every page. The status bar shows the mode like a text editor does, so you always know what a key will do.

Works with your assistant

Connect any MCP client with a token of its own. The MCP tools for assistants read and change your todos, notes and people, and every change shows up in the history with the token's name. Read-only tokens are possible too.

Private and work, apart

Keep separate accounts with nothing shared between them, and switch in one click. Each browser tab shows one account, so two tabs can show both at once.

  • Privateannacurrent Workanna-work
  • Sign in with a password and an authenticator code, or with a passkey.
  • Recovery codes for the day your phone is gone.
  • Export everything as a zip at any time: data to restore from, plus one Markdown file per todo.
